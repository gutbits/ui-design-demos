💡 How Harmonies Work

  • Complementary: Opposite on the wheel (180°) — high contrast, vibrant.
  • Analogous: Neighbors (±30°) — harmonious, serene.
  • Triadic: Evenly spaced (120° apart) — balanced, colorful.
  • Split-Complementary: Base + two adjacent to its complement — less tension than complementary.
  • Tetradic: Four colors in two complementary pairs — rich but needs careful balance.
  • Monochromatic: Same hue, different saturation/lightness — elegant and cohesive.
